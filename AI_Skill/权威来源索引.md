# 权威来源索引（国际前沿对齐的唯一权威清单）

## A 级 — 顶级学术会议/期刊论文

按主题分组（会议/年份为引用必备项）：

### 缓存替换算法
- Che, Wang, Tung, "Analysis and Design of Hierarchical Web Caching Systems", IEEE INFOCOM 2003
- Megiddo & Modha, "ARC: A Self-Tuning, Low Overhead Replacement Cache", FAST 2003
- Johnson & Shasha, "2Q: A Low Overhead High Performance Buffer Management Replacement Algorithm", VLDB 1994
- Karedla, Love & Wherry, "Caching Strategies to Improve Disk System Performance", IEEE Computer 1994（SLRU）
- Jiang, Zhou et al., "CLOCK-Pro: An Effective Improvement of the CLOCK Replacement", USENIX ATC 2005
- Einziger et al., "TinyLFU: A Highly Efficient Cache Admission Policy", IEEE ICDCS 2017
- Beckmann et al., "LHD: Improving Cache Hit Rate by Maximizing Hit Density", NSDI 2018
- Yang et al., "FIFO Queues are All You Need for Cache Eviction" (S3-FIFO), SOSP 2023
- Zhang et al., "SIEVE is Simpler than LRU", USENIX NSDI 2024
- Vietri et al., "Driving Cache Replacement with ML-based LeCaR", USENIX HotCloud 2018
- Rodriguez et al., "Learning from Optimized Data" (CACHEUS), FAST 2021
- Song et al., "Learning to Recycle for Cache Replacement" (LRB), 2020（arXiv:2010.07814）
- Jain & Lin, "Hawkeye: Cache Replacement Policy with Deep Learning", ISCA 2016（Hawkeye 提出于该组；后续 DAC'17）
- Zhou et al., "3L-Cache", FAST 2025
- Karger et al., "Consistent Hashing and Random Trees", STOC 1997
- Thaler & Ravishankar, "Using Name-Based Mappings to Increase Hit Rates", IEEE TPDS 1998（Rendezvous/HRW）

### 系统/OS/存储
- O'Neil et al., "The LRU-K Page Replacement Algorithm for Database Disk Buffering", SIGMOD 1993
- Zhao et al., Multi-Generational LRU (MGLRU), Linux 6.1 合入（2022），论文报告 2021
- Seo et al., "DAMON: Data Access Monitoring Framework", 合入 Linux 5.15（2021）
- Kwon et al., "ChameleonDB/键值存储" 等 ZNS 谱系；Matias Bjørling, "ZNS: Avoiding the Block Interface Tax"（Fast/SOSP 谱系）
- Corbett et al., "Spanner", OSDI 2012（时间戳并发控制参照）
- Ongaro & Ousterhout, "In Search of an Understandable Consensus Algorithm" (Raft), USENIX ATC 2014
- Lamport, "Paxos Made Simple", ACM SIGACT News 2001
- Nishtala et al., "Scaling Memcache at Facebook", NSDI 2013
- Yang et al., "A Large Scale Analysis of Hundreds of In-memory Cache Clusters at Twitter", OSDI 2020

### LLM 推理缓存
- Kwon et al., "Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention" (vLLM), SOSP 2023
- Zheng et al., "SGLang: Efficient Execution of Structured Language Model Programs"（RadixAttention）, NeurIPS 2024
- Qin et al., "Mooncake: A KVCache-centric Disaggregated Architecture for LLM Serving", FAST 2025
- Sheng et al., "FlexGen" (IO-aware), ICML 2023；H2O (ICLR 2024)；StreamingLLM (ICLR 2024)；SnapKV
- DeepSeek, "DeepSeek-V2" (MLA), 2024

### 缓存安全
- Yarom & Falkner, "FLUSH+RELOAD: A High Resolution, Low Noise, L3 Cache Side-Channel Attack", USENIX Security 2014
- Lipp et al., "Meltdown", USENIX Security 2018；Kocher et al., "Spectre", IEEE S&P 2019
- Wilke et al., "CacheWarp: Software-based Fault Attack on AMD SEV", USENIX Security 2023
- Nguyen & Touimi（CPDoS 原始披露 2019），Hoai et al., "CPDoS: Cache-Poisoned Denial-of-Service Attack", USENIX Security 2020
